Developed a Double DQN–DE evolutionary reinforcement learning framework that adaptively selects mutation strategies and control parameters for multi-UAV (unmanned aerial vehicle) path planning. Designed a composite reward mechanism balancing convergence improvement, population diversity, and feasibility under complex terrain and threat constraints. Validated on LiDAR-based real-world island terrain, achieving up to 27.7% improvement over DE and pure Double DQN baselines in path cost and robustness.
