Integrates a parallel snake-optimizer-tuned kernel extreme learning machine (Parallel SO-KELM) into wind power forecasting models, enabling automated hyperparameter tuning with improved adaptability to nonlinear, non-stationary wind power data. Includes parameter-sensitivity analysis and stability evaluation across multiple experimental runs on real wind power datasets.
