Contributed to the implementation and debugging of a hierarchical surrogate-assisted optimization framework — hybrid differential evolution with surrogate-model parameter tuning and EMA-based dynamic population control for global–local search. Assisted in designing the Chen hyperchaotic system and DNA-encoding fusion workflow for encryption, and performed multi-dataset encryption tests with pixel-correlation analysis and attack-resistance evaluation.
